# Temporal Modeling — Applied Playbook

## TL;DR

- **Triton cannot reproduce its own history, and the three blockers are independent — fixing any two still leaves "what did the leaderboard say on 1 July" unanswerable.** `pitches` has no arrival time, `pitch_baselines` has no timestamp and is destructively upserted, `league_averages` is `DELETE` then re-`INSERT`. (computed)
- **No version column exists anywhere on the platform.** A grep for `baseline_version|metric_version|as_of|valid_from|sys_period` across `scripts/`, `lib/` and `app/` returns zero hits on 2026-08-22. Nothing records which vintage produced which number. (computed)
- **The only surviving baseline vintage in the repo is an accident and it is five months stale.** `BL_BY_YEAR` is 147 lines, every one blamed to 2026-03-14; `BL_BY_YEAR[2026]` was therefore built before the 2026 regular season opened, and it is the client-side Stuff+ fallback shown under the same label as the DB column. (computed)
- **The cheapest sufficient fix is not bitemporality — it is versioning two small dimension tables and shipping an honesty label.** 206 baseline rows and ~1,806 league-average rows per vintage, against 8.88M immutable fact rows. (computed)
- **ET discipline exists and is not universal.** `ymdInTimeZone()` is imported by 9 files; the UTC-truncating `toISOString().slice(0,10)` idiom appears at 39 sites across 27 files, one of which is a live off-by-a-day in the broadcast path. (computed)
- **Savant restatement is visible in the data and structurally invisible to the ingest.** `ST`/`SV` carry `MIN(game_year)=2015`; retired `FT` reappeared in 2026 with 13 rows; the 3-day window gives ~55–64 h of post-game looks and never revisits a closed date. (computed)
- **The MiLB event seam is an ingest-time seam, so no `game_date` predicate separates it** — and it does not merely add noise: a full-2026 MiLB K% is *diluted by the pre-June share* because the numerator is vocabulary-sensitive and the denominator is not. (computed)
- **The 46-day stale denominator is an as-of mismatch inside a single number, not a data gap.** Numerator moved, denominator froze on 2026-06-26. Those values are correct as-of June and mislabelled as current — annotate, do not restate. (computed)
- **A written restatement policy is free, and it is the one control Triton has never had.** BLS never revises the unadjusted CPI; BEA revises quarterly and archives every vintage. Both are defensible; having neither is not. (established)

## The deliverable: reproduce the 2026 command leaderboard as of 1 July

Everything below is sequenced around one concrete task — re-render what `/api/leaderboard-triton` returned for `{gameYear: 2026, mode: 'plus', sortBy: 'cmd_plus'}` on 2026-07-01. Six things must be pinned. Four are gone.

| Pin | Where it lives | State today | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Qualification** | `minPitches` is a request parameter, defaulted to 500 at `app/api/leaderboard-triton/route.ts:27` | Never stored, never echoed in the response | **Lost, free to fix** |
| **Command inputs** | `pitcher_season_command` filtered `game_year = Y` at `app/api/leaderboard-triton/route.ts:57-59` | Upserted in place per pitcher × pitch type × year; July's values overwritten | **Lost** |
| **Stuff+ column** | `AVG(stuff_plus)` over `pitches` at `app/api/leaderboard-triton/route.ts:63-68` | A patchwork of nightly baseline vintages with no version column; ~249k Jun–Aug rows were rescored on 2026-08-11 | **Lost** |
| **Population** | "Which rows existed on 2026-07-01" | Unanswerable — `pitches` carries no `created_at`/`updated_at` | **Lost; proxy available** |
| **Tie order** | Neither query carries an `ORDER BY`; the JS sort has no tiebreaker at `app/api/leaderboard-triton/route.ts:166-173`, over values rounded to one decimal at `:144-149` | Ties resolve by whatever order the planner returned | **Lost, free to fix** |
| **Serving layer** | 30-minute in-process cache at `app/api/leaderboard-triton/route.ts:9-10,42-47` | Keyed on request params, never invalidated by a rescore | **Lost, free to fix** |

**The minimum sufficient version** is not a bitemporal rebuild. It is: version the two small dimension tables (items 2 and 3), pin the parameters (item 8), use `game_date <= D` as the *population* proxy with a published lateness bound (item 1), and leave `pitches` alone. That buys byte-identical replay for any date **after the archive starts**.

**What it does not buy.** It cannot reconstruct 2026-07-01, because the vintages that produced it no longer exist — item 1 is the only honest answer for dates already past. It cannot detect a Savant revision that has already been absorbed (item 7 starts that clock). And it cannot recover the batted-ball granularity that the MiLB event map collapsed (item 5).

## NOW (0–6 weeks)

**1. Publish the reproducibility horizon before fixing any mechanism.** Rung 0 costs nothing and is undone. Every metric × date range gets one of four labels — *bit-reproducible*, *reproducible with a bounded caveat*, *approximately reproducible*, *not reproducible* — carried in `docs/VARIABLES.md` and rendered next to the value. The measured caveat already exists: Feb–May 2026 rows carry **+0.29 to +0.59** points of upward Stuff+ bias against current baselines, while June and August read exactly 0.000 because the 2026-08-11 repair overwrote their vintages. The range that replays perfectly is the one whose history died. Rendering is Cas's (`Cas/analytics-ux/`); the labels are Li's. *Stop condition:* every plus-stat in `lib/reportMetrics.ts:25-60` carries a label, and no unlabelled metric ships. (estimated)

**2. Start the baseline archive tonight — before item 3, before anything else.** Prepend an archive `INSERT` to `refreshPitchBaselines` at `app/api/update/route.ts:249-277`, writing today's `pitch_baselines` into a history table keyed `(pitch_name, game_year, as_of_date)` before the destructive `ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE` at `app/api/update/route.ts:269-276` runs. Cost: 206 rows/night, ~26 MB/season — **0.27%** of the 9,711 MB `pitches` table. It is first because every night it does not run is a vintage that never existed. *Stop condition:* a stored `stuff_plus` for any date at or after the archive start recomputes from retained inputs to within ±0.001 on three sampled pitches. (computed)

**3. Make `league_averages` append-only — but only after Jo's timeout fix lands.** `scripts/create-refresh-league-averages.sql:49` opens with `DELETE FROM league_averages WHERE season = p_season`, so `updated_at` records when the previous benchmark was destroyed, not what it was. Add `as_of_date` to the key and append. Cost ≈616 kB/day ≈ 225 MB/season, **2.3%** of `pitches`. The dependency is real: an append-only refresh that times out leaves a *partial vintage* in the archive, worse than a destructive one that times out and leaves yesterday intact. The refresh chain has been dead since 2026-06-26 — see `Jo/applied/postgres-performance-applied.md`. *Stop condition:* two consecutive successful nightly runs produce two distinct `as_of_date` rows per (season, level, role, metric), and the older one is still readable. (computed)

**4. Fix the two live ET/UTC defects, then make the discipline enforceable rather than remembered.** `app/api/scene-stats/route.ts:619-621` derives "yesterday" as `new Date()` minus a day, truncated through `toISOString()` — on a UTC server that is UTC-yesterday, so from 20:00 ET to midnight it returns **today's** ET games under a "yesterday" label, live on broadcast overlays. Separately, `app/api/update/route.ts:287-292` defines a private `addDaysUtc` that duplicates `addDaysToYmd` in `lib/dateTz.ts:27-32` — the ingest is the one place that most needs the shared helper and it forked instead. The correct pattern is already in the MiLB cron at `app/api/cron/milb-pitches/route.ts:12-22`. *Stop condition:* an ESLint `no-restricted-syntax` rule bans `toISOString().slice(0,10)` and `.split('T')[0]` under `app/api/**` and `lib/**` with an explicit allowlist, the count falls from 39 sites, and a test at 23:00 ET asserts `scene-stats` returns the ET-yesterday slate. (computed)

**5. Rule that 2026 season-to-date MiLB rate stats are not a single statistic, and stop serving them as one.** `lib/reportQueryBuilder.ts:1,113` applies the MLB-lowercase SQL in `lib/reportMetrics.ts` verbatim to `milb_pitches` via `app/api/milb/report/route.ts:9`. The consequences are asymmetric, not merely noisy: `k_pct` at `lib/reportMetrics.ts:25` matches `events LIKE '%strikeout%'` — post-June rows only — over an `events IS NOT NULL` denominator that counts both vocabularies, so the rate is **diluted by exactly the pre-June share** (~46.5% of 2026 rows on Jo's count). `ba` at `lib/reportMetrics.ts:32` is wrong in both directions at once — the numerator misses pre-June `Single` while the denominator fails to exclude pre-June `Walk` — and `ip` at `lib/reportMetrics.ts:10` counts no pre-June out at all. **Do not offer a `game_date` cutoff as the fix.** `EVENT_NORMALIZE_MAP` at `app/api/update/milb/route.ts:75-99` is applied at write time at `:245`, `syncMilbPitches` accepts an arbitrary range, and no column records when a row was last written — so no event-time predicate is *guaranteed* to separate the vocabularies. Storage repair is Jo's (`Jo/applied/data-quality-applied.md`). *Stop condition:* a 2026 MiLB range spanning 2026-06-08 returns a refusal, not a number, until `count(DISTINCT events) = count(DISTINCT lower(replace(events,' ','_')))` holds for the season. (computed)

**6. Rule on the 46-day stale denominator: annotate, do not restate.** Since 2026-06-26 every plus-stat has divided a moving numerator by a frozen population. That is not staleness in the ordinary sense — it is an as-of mismatch *inside one number*, where the two halves are correct as of different dates. Name it that way. The ruling is to leave the values and label them, on two grounds: the measured magnitude of the analogous vintage effect is sub-point on a 100-scale (item 1), well under the display grain; and a recompute would erase the only remaining evidence of what users were actually shown. *Stop condition:* every surface reading `league_averages` also reads its `updated_at` and renders it, and no plus-stat renders without a denominator date. Rendering is Cas's. (estimated)

## NEXT (6 weeks – 6 months)

**7. Adopt an annual settled-season diff as the re-ingest policy, and do not widen the nightly window.** The 3-day re-sync at `app/api/cron/pitches/route.ts:36-38` heals provider *lateness* — it is why the ingest survives with almost no retry layer — but it gives ~55–64 h of post-game looks, and ordinary publication lag already spends most of that. Anything Savant revises later never arrives, and `pitch_name` is the join key to the baselines at `app/api/update/route.ts:329-330`, so a late reclassification is scored against the wrong baseline permanently. Reclassification is not speculative: `ST` and `SV` carry `MIN(game_year)=2015`, which the 2015 taxonomy cannot produce, and retired `FT` reappears in 2026 with 13 rows. Policy, in three parts: (a) leave the nightly window at 3 days; (b) once per off-season, re-pull a sample of settled dates, diff on `(game_pk, at_bat_number, pitch_number)`, and store **only changed cells** — roughly 20k ledger rows per season against 8.9M facts; (c) promote the existing `new_pitch_names` integrity check from `warn` to `fail` in `app/api/cron/integrity/route.ts:26-35`, since it is the only detector in this class that already exists and it has warned 54 times without anyone acting. *Stop condition:* a restatement ledger exists with at least one settled season diffed and a published per-column change rate. If `pitch_name` moves more than 0.5%, Stuff+ history is materially wrong and item 12 becomes live. (computed)

**8. Make the leaderboard deterministic, then snapshot values rather than ranks.** Determinism first, because snapshotting a non-deterministic result stores noise. Add `ORDER BY pitcher` to both queries at `app/api/leaderboard-triton/route.ts:50-60,63-68`, add `pitcher` as the terminal sort key at `app/api/leaderboard-triton/route.ts:166-173`, and echo `{gameYear, gameType, minPitches, sortBy, mode}` in the response body. Ties are common, not exotic — `cmd_plus` is rounded to one decimal at `app/api/leaderboard-triton/route.ts:144-149` *before* the sort. Then snapshot: a rank alone is nearly worthless, because rank is a whole-population function of a qualification rule, so a threshold change reads as a performance change. Store the value with `n_qualified`, `leader_value` and `qual_floor` beside it — `league_averages` already carries all four and is one as-of key short of being a snapshot table. *Stop condition:* the same request issued twice across a cache flush returns byte-identical rows. (computed)

**9. Add the `game_year` ↔ `game_date` invariant as the ninth integrity check.** Three definitions of "season" run in one nightly chain and nothing ties them together: `pitch_baselines` groups by `game_year` at `app/api/update/route.ts:267-268`; `refresh_league_averages` windows on `game_date` in `[Jan 1 y, Jan 1 y+1)` at `scripts/create-refresh-league-averages.sql:47-48`; and the Stuff+ UPDATE joins on `p.game_year = b.game_year` while filtering on `p.game_date` at `app/api/update/route.ts:329-331`. A row whose `game_year` disagrees with its `game_date` is silently scored against another year's baseline, or against none — and it is left NULL with no error. The columns have different provenance on the two fact tables: MLB copies `game_year` straight from the Savant CSV, while MiLB derives it at `app/api/update/milb/route.ts:165` with a `new Date().getFullYear()` fallback that reads the server's **UTC** year. *Stop condition:* `count(*) WHERE game_year <> extract(year from game_date)` is asserted zero on both fact tables, and the check returns `fail`, not `warn`. (computed)

**10. Reconcile the two Stuff+ vintages, which are five months apart and share one label.** `BL_BY_YEAR` at `lib/leagueStats.ts:1006-1150` is a checked-in snapshot of `pitch_baselines`; `git blame` puts all 147 lines at 2026-03-14 and nothing has touched them since. `BL_BY_YEAR[2026]` at `lib/leagueStats.ts:1139` was therefore computed before the 2026 regular season began, and `STUFF_ZSCORE_BASELINES` at `lib/leagueStats.ts:1154` makes it the fallback for any unrecognised year, while `getStuffBaseline` at `lib/leagueStats.ts:1159-1169` substitutes the *nearest* year for a missing pitch type. `lib/pitcherStats.ts:290-292` then picks between the DB column and this client value on a 50%-coverage threshold and labels both "Stuff+" — so which vintage a user sees depends on a coverage number that varies by era and moved during the 2026 outage. *Stop condition:* either the client fallback is deleted, or it is served from a versioned endpoint whose response carries the vintage date, and `lib/pitcherStats.ts` reports which source produced the number. (computed)

**11. Give the wRC+ and FIP-family constants a time dimension.** `PARK_FACTORS` at `lib/constants-data.ts:25-30` is a single 2024 vintage applied to 2015–2026 by an explicit comment, and wRC+ is computed at query time rather than stored — so editing a constant restates twelve seasons with no trace. It has already happened: wRC+ moved 5–6 points on 2026-05-08. The SQL side has the same defect with a sharper edge: `scripts/create-refresh-league-averages.sql:51-62` hardcodes cFIP, league ERA, wOBA, wOBA scale and HR/FB for **2015–2024 only**, and the `ELSE` at `scripts/create-refresh-league-averages.sql:62` applies the 2025 constants to 2025, 2026 and every future season — while `lib/constants-data.ts:22` already carries genuine 2026 values that disagree with it. One platform, two answers, no version. *Stop condition:* both sides read one vintage-tagged source, and changing it requires a version bump recorded in `docs/VARIABLES.md`. (computed)

## LATER (6+ months)

**12. Write the restatement policy down, and default to *restate forward, never backward*.** Triton has rescored its fact table twice in four months and has no rule governing either event. Serious publishers disagree — BLS never revises the unadjusted CPI, BEA archives every vintage — and both survive audit because the rule is published. Default here: rescore forward only and record the seam, on the 2026-08-11 evidence — the Feb–May vintage bias is +0.29 to +0.59 points, roughly half a percent of a 100-scale value and well below the display grain, so a full historical rescore buys accuracy nobody can see while destroying the last evidence of what the vintages were. Restatement is a real option; this is the case where it is wrong. *Stop condition:* `docs/VARIABLES.md` carries a restatement section, and every rescore writes a ledger row with date range, code revision, and the baseline as-of it used. (established)

**13. Only after items 2, 3 and 7 land: evaluate a `pitches` history table — and expect to decline it.** The cost is ≈2.33M history rows and ≈1.3 GB of heap per season, most of it recording that nothing changed, because the 3-day re-sync rewrites each row about four times with `ignoreDuplicates: false` at `app/api/update/route.ts:148`. Item 7's diff ledger answers the same question at ~20k rows per season. Postgres offers no shortcut: there is no `PERIOD FOR`, no `WITH SYSTEM VERSIONING`, no `FOR SYSTEM_TIME AS OF`; PG 18 added `WITHOUT OVERLAPS` keys, `FOR PORTION OF` lands in PG 19, and Supabase's default is PG 17 — so the portable construction is a `tstzrange` plus a GiST exclusion constraint, which by design makes the existing destructive upsert *unable to run*. Adopt only if item 7 measures `pitch_name` restatement above 0.5% per season. See `Li/temporal-modeling/02-bitemporal-modeling.md` for the full costing. (computed)

## Standing Rules

1. **Never derive a baseball calendar date from an instant without naming a time zone.** Use `ymdInTimeZone()` at `lib/dateTz.ts:10-18` or a `date` column. MLB's game date is *scheduled*, not computed — no rule recovers it from a UTC instant, and a UTC-derived date misdates the home night games of most of the league.
2. **An `updated_at` is a stamp, not a history.** If a refresh deletes before it inserts, that column records when the previous answer was destroyed. Five Triton tables carry one and all five overwrite in place.
3. **Version the small mutable dimensions; recompute the big immutable facts.** This one rule assigns every table on the platform without further argument, and it is why items 2 and 3 come before item 13.
4. **A seam that exists at ingest time cannot be filtered at event time.** This is the whole content of the MiLB vocabulary problem, and the reason a `game_date` cutoff is not a fix.
5. **Restate forward, annotate backward.** Recomputing history erases the only evidence of what was shown, and the measured error it corrects is smaller than the display grain.
6. **Publish the horizon before repairing the mechanism.** A known-unanswerable question is a managed risk; an unknowingly-unanswerable one is a wrong answer with a confident face.
7. **The attractive wrong move, named and killed: widening the nightly re-sync from 3 days to 14.** It fails on three independent grounds. It multiplies the ingest upsert against a statement timeout already at 94.8% of its cap. It still cannot see a revision to a 2019 game — the actual restatement risk. And because the Stuff+ UPDATE carries no `stuff_plus IS NULL` guard at `app/api/update/route.ts:320-333`, a wider window rescores a wider span every night — making the vintage patchwork worse in the name of fixing it. Item 7's annual diff costs less and answers the real question.
