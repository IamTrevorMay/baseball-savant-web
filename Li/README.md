## Domain 2: Statistical Inference & Uncertainty (`statistical-inference/`)
- [x] [01-sampling-and-sample-size.md](statistical-inference/01-sampling-and-sample-size.md) — **The design-effect doc.** Why a census still has sampling error (design- vs model-based inference, FPC); why the n that matters is the metric's own event count, not the pitch count; SE of a mean vs a proportion; **clustering** — at ~75 pitches/start and ρ ≈ 0.02–0.04, deff = 2.5–3.9, so Povich's 149 pitches behave like 38–60, and for an *outing-level* effect the real n is **2**. Full Povich arithmetic: SE 0.53 → 0.75–1.07, SE_diff 1.1–1.5, the 2.3-point move at **1.5–2.1 SE** (prior 1.3–2.1 reproduced, top end corrected), against an **MDE of 3.0–4.2 points** — the sample could not have detected the move it appears to show. Plus the vintage-seam **bias** that no n removes, non-stationarity as a *ceiling* on useful n, and the answer to "how many pitches": **~415 per side, 5–6 starts, to detect 2 Stuff+ points at 80% power.** Ends with runnable ICC/σ SQL. **Anti-recommendation: no minimum-sample gate.**
- [x] [02-reliability-stabilization.md](statistical-inference/02-reliability-stabilization.md) — **The consumption-side stabilization doc**: given a value already on screen, is it defensible? Split-half, Spearman–Brown, Cronbach's α/KR-21, and the identity `r = n/(n+k)` that makes a stabilization point *the same parameter* as a shrinkage constant — hence **n(α=0.7) = 2.33 × n(α=0.5)**, the conversion that makes Carleton-lineage and Driveline figures non-comparable as published. Carleton's method and its four limits (threshold convention, population-conditionality, survivorship, and **silence on bias**). Separates **reliability** from **precision** criteria — the two things the literature calls "stabilization." Estimates Triton Stuff+ at ~25–60 pitches per pitch type, shows the aggregate has no threshold at all (mix-weighted), and rules reliever command cells structurally below every published band. Povich at 149 pitches is **above** threshold and the comparison is **still** indefensible. Proposes `MetricDef.stabilizesAt` (Cas implements the surface). Cross-references and extends `Soto/algorithm-design/09-model-validation-stabilization.md`.
- [x] [03-regression-to-mean-shrinkage.md](statistical-inference/03-regression-to-mean-shrinkage.md) — Regression to the mean, empirical Bayes, James–Stein, shrinkage targets, how much to regress a two-start sample.
- [x] [04-uncertainty-quantification.md](statistical-inference/04-uncertainty-quantification.md) — **The Stuff+ error-propagation doc — nobody had done it.** Confidence vs credible intervals (worked: the same two-start 108 is "significantly above average" frequentist and "can't tell" Bayesian); SEs for means/proportions/ratios (Wilson, not Wald); the delta method giving Var(ẑ) ≈ 1/N + z²/2N — so **baseline error grows with |z| and never shrinks with the pitcher's n**; the outing-cluster bootstrap. Full Stuff+ derivation: the **covariance term stated explicitly** (σ_S moves 5.67→7.04 across plausible ρ, ±20% on every interval), the correction that a *pitcher's* per-pitch SD is **≈2.8, not the league's 6.04**, a four-term variance table (pitch-to-pitch / baseline μ̂ / baseline σ̂ / vintage bias), the clamp as a **σ-collapse alarm rather than a truncation problem** (16.6 SD to bind), and the missing `pitch_count` floor. **Punchline: at full-season n the sampling interval (±0.30) is smaller than the vintage bias (≈0.5) — the binding constraint is provenance, not sample size — and Triton currently displays no interval anywhere and a sample size in exactly one place.** Ends with when an interval misleads more than none, and an anti-recommendation against shipping error bars before `baseline_version`.
- [ ] [05-multiple-comparisons-leaderboards.md](statistical-inference/05-multiple-comparisons-leaderboards.md) — Multiplicity on leaderboards and dashboards, FDR/Bonferroni, the "most improved pitcher" trap, post-hoc selection.
- [ ] [06-confounding-selection-survivorship.md](statistical-inference/06-confounding-selection-survivorship.md) — Confounding, selection and survivorship bias, injury/demotion censoring, park and opponent confounds, causal-vs-correlational discipline.
- [x] [07-trend-detection-changepoints.md](statistical-inference/07-trend-detection-changepoints.md) — **"Did it actually change?" — the direct answer to the Povich question, whose first answer was wrong three ways.** A four-gate protocol: **coverage** (Aug 2026 `stuff_plus` was 0/36,621 → Jo), **comparability** (May and Aug straddle the 2026-08-11 rescore seam: ±0.3–0.6 pts, ~26% of the 2.3-pt move), **magnitude-vs-noise** (149 pitches over 2 starts is **2 clusters**; deff 2–4, `n_eff` 37–75, MDE ≈2.5–3.5 pts vs an observed 2.3 — underpowered before it ran), and **corroboration** — the gate that decides, because Stuff+ has no release-position term so the arm-slot drop is *independent* evidence. Method table (CUSUM/PELT/binseg/Bayesian CPD) with the grain argument that kills in-season detection at ~30 outings/season; Mann–Kendall/Sen with the autocorrelation corrections and the Sen-interval trap; step-vs-drift via the CUSUM path; **changed pitcher vs changed measurement** (Savant's retro-relabelled `pitch_type` re-buckets a pitch into a different `pitch_baselines` row); league-wide steps (2023 rules, sweeper) fixed by a difference-in-differences control; multiplicity across players × metrics × candidate changepoints. **Anti-recommendation: do not build a nightly all-players changepoint scanner** — it fails gate 1 by construction.

- [ ] [09-small-sample-communication.md](statistical-inference/09-small-sample-communication.md) — Reporting an unstable number honestly, hedging that still informs, when to refuse to show a number at all.
- [ ] [10-benchmarking-percentiles.md](statistical-inference/10-benchmarking-percentiles.md) — Percentile methodology, population definition, tie handling, percentile instability at the tails, rank vs value comparison.
